Who We Are

Sree Raghavendra Multispeciality Hospital & IVF Centre

Established in 1994, Sree Raghavendra Multispeciality Hospital has been serving patients with compassionate and advanced healthcare services in Hyderabad.

In 2017, the hospital expanded into advanced fertility and IVF treatments, helping couples achieve their dream of parenthood with modern reproductive care.

Our hospital combines experienced doctors, advanced diagnostics, maternity care, emergency support and patient-focused treatment under one roof.
